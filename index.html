Profile

What I actually do

I'm an Assistant Product Manager and Lead Business Analyst who spends most of my time moving a digital product from a rough idea to something a few million people actually use — across reinsurance, automotive, healthcare and retail.

Day to day that's product roadmaps, PRDs, T-shirt sizing, sprint and release planning, and a lot of translation between engineering, QA, design and the business. I've led end-to-end delivery for global automotive brands including Škoda and Audi, rebuilt a legacy reinsurance system for Reinsurance Group of America, and introduced AI-assisted catalog tooling that cut manual product work by 80%.

I work well where a platform has outgrown its old system — migrating data, untangling integrations, and getting a cross-functional team to ship on a hard deadline without losing what made the old thing work.
